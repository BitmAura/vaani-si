How VANNI Operates Behind the Scenes

A transparent engineering look at how autonomous regional voice AI answers customer phone calls, adapts to Kannada and Kanglish dialects, and locks CRM appointments in under 500ms.

The Autonomous Telephony Loop

From Inbound Phone Call to WhatsApp CRM Booking in Under 3 Seconds

VANNI is not an IVR, a chatbot, or a generic English voice assistant. It is a full-stack, regional voice employee that pairs carrier-grade Indian SIP telephony with local vernacular dialect understanding (North Karnataka, Mysuru, Bangalore Kanglish, and Hindi) and deterministic business knowledge retrieval.

The 4-Stage Lifecycle

Zero-Drop Regional Voice Execution

How customer inquiries move from acoustic sound waves to verified business outcomes.

01

Multi-Modal Business Brain Ingestion & Guardrails

LAYER 1: DETERMINISTIC FACTS

You provide your hospital doctor rosters, real estate plot master plans, clinic policies, or website URLs. VANNI ingests, validates, and hashes your business data into an isolated Business Brain bounded by strict mathematical guardrails.

Conflict Engine:
Auto-flags pricing/timing contradictions before live deployment.
Zero-Hallucination Bound:
If a fact isn't in your catalog, the AI safely defers instead of guessing.
Vernacular Synonym Mapping:
Understands "Root Canal", "Danta Chikitse", and "Teeth Pain".
02

Sovereign Telephony & Sub-50ms SIP Routing

LAYER 2: CARRIER TELECOM

VANNI provisions a dedicated institutional Indian virtual landline (+91 80 DID for Bengaluru) or mobile number, or connects directly to your existing office phone via carrier conditional call forwarding.

Indian SIP Interconnect:
Licensed Airtel / Jio telecom trunks with local MeitY-compliant SBCs.
Duplex Audio Streaming:
Bidirectional 16kHz PCM audio stream via TLS/SRTP encrypted WebSockets.
Instant Barge-In:
Acoustic VAD mutes AI within 40ms when caller speaks or interrupts.
03

Streaming Vernacular STT, Dialect Detection & Neural Voice

LAYER 3: VERNACULAR AI PIPELINE

As the caller speaks, Sarvam Saaras AI converts regional audio into text tokens. Our real-time dialect classifier automatically identifies whether the caller is speaking North Kannada (Hubli/Belagavi), Kanglish Business, Mysuru Kannada, or Hindi/Hinglish, adjusting vocabulary and cadence dynamically.

Dialect-Matched Fillers:
Plays "Haudri...", "Sure guru...", or "Ji bilkul..." in 120ms to eliminate dead air.
Semantic Turn Endpointing:
Waits if caller says "matte..." or "nodri...", responds instantly on questions.
Sarvam Bulbul Neural TTS:
Lifelike regional speech with natural warmth and true Kannada phonetics.
04

Real-Time Action Execution & Instant WhatsApp Telemetry

LAYER 4: REVENUE & CRM DISPATCH

The AI locks appointment slots in your calendar, computes lead qualification scores (Hot / Warm / Cold), and dispatches a verified bilingual summary directly to your business WhatsApp in under 3 seconds after call completion.

Calendar Lock:
Direct Google Calendar & Outlook 365 2-way slot confirmation.
WhatsApp Lead Dossier:
Bilingual summary with caller name, budget, intent, and audio link.
CRM Webhook Push:
Instantly updates LeadSquared, Zoho, Salesforce, or Google Sheets.
