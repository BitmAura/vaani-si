# VANNI Web — Authenticated SaaS Portal (/app)

This route group houses the authenticated client SaaS dashboard and telemetry operating system:

- `/app/dashboard`: Operational KPIs, live call monitors, and outcome telemetry.
- `/app/agents`: Configured AI employees, dialects, and status controls.
- `/app/calls`: Complete call table, CDR inspection drawers, audio players, and verified transcripts.
- `/app/leads`: Structured customer dossiers, requirements, and CRM sync status.
- `/app/campaigns`: Outbound call dispatch, lead lists, and retry parameters.
- `/app/appointments`: Scheduled consultations, site visits, and calendar bindings.
- `/app/knowledge`: Business Brain document management, FAQ ingestion, and guardrails.
- `/app/integrations`: Open-source CRM (Twenty, Chatwoot, Cal.com) and webhook configurations.
- `/app/settings`: Virtual phone numbers, SIP trunking, team access, and billing.
