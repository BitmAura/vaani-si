# VANNI Web — (public) Routes

This route group houses all public marketing, vertical solution, and acquisition pages:

- `/`: Master Landing Page with 17-section narrative, live call proof, and conversion modal.
- `/features`: Platform architecture, audio streaming specs, and CRM handoffs.
- `/how-it-works`: 5-step operational workflow from business knowledge to call handling.
- `/industries`: Directory of vertical specializations.
- `/industries/dental`: Dental practice appointment coordination & receptionist.
- `/industries/healthcare`: Hospital OPD triage, doctor schedules & emergency guidelines.
- `/industries/ivf`: Empathetic, confidential fertility inquiry handling.
- `/industries/hospitality`: Weekend getaway, resort booking & banquet inquiry concierge.
- `/industries/real-estate`: Speed-to-lead outbound qualification & Sunday site visit booking.
- `/pricing`: Transparent monthly platform fees and talk minute allocations.
- `/demo`: Direct phone-call demonstration trigger.
- `/contact`: Solutions engineering and enterprise sales inquiries.
- `/security`: Multi-tenant data isolation, PostgreSQL RLS, and privacy policies.
