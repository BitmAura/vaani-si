# VANNI AI Employee / Agent Builder — Overview

## Core Value Proposition

VANNI rejects the generic paradigm of "building a chatbot". Instead, VANNI provides a comprehensive, enterprise-grade **AI Employee Architecture** engineered specifically for Karnataka and Indian business enterprises.

An **AI Employee** is an autonomous operational role (e.g., AI Receptionist, AI Lead Qualification Executive, AI Appointment Coordinator, AI Sales Assistant) configured on top of:
- **Business Brain**: Authoritative locations, services, operating hours, doctors/staff, and pricing.
- **Knowledge Engine**: Multi-format unstructured documents, bilingual FAQs, and semantic vector retrieval.
- **Tools & Capabilities**: Calendar scheduling, telephony transfer, and lead CRM dossiers.
- **Policies & Safety Guardrails**: Zero medical diagnosis, zero real estate price fabrication, and non-deceptive AI identification.
- **Runtime Snapshot Compiler**: Immutable session compilation enforcing a strict 10-level instruction precedence hierarchy.

## High-Level Topology

```
Organization (Multi-Tenant)
   │
   ├── Business Brain (Authoritative Facts, Staff, Services)
   │
   ├── Knowledge Engine (PDFs, Documents, FAQs, Chunks)
   │
   └── AI Employees (Workforce Directory)
           │
           ├── Identity (Name, Role, Introduction, Non-Deceptive Disclosure)
           ├── Objective (Primary mission, secondary objectives, success criteria)
           ├── Capabilities (Server-enforced tool execution permissions)
           ├── Personality (Preset, tone, conversational style, energy)
           ├── Language (Kannada, English, Kanglish blend, switching rules)
           ├── Knowledge Access (Category filters, source restrictions)
           ├── Tools (Calendar, CRM, telephony transfer, communications)
           ├── Policies & Guardrails (Healthcare, real estate, zero hallucination)
           ├── Escalation Rules (Customer requests human, emergency casualty routing)
           ├── State Machine (DRAFT → TESTING → ACTIVE ↔ PAUSED → ARCHIVED)
           └── Versioning (Immutable snapshots v1, v2 with zero-downtime rollback)
```

## Supported Roles
- **AI Receptionist**: Clinic timings, doctor consultation schedules, appointments, emergency routing.
- **AI Lead Qualifier**: Budget verification, timeline discovery, requirement qualification.
- **AI Appointment Coordinator**: Schedule, confirm, reschedule, and verify doctor/specialist availability.
- **AI Sales Assistant**: Real estate layout approvals, amenities, plot dimensions, bank loans, Sunday site visit cabs.
- **AI Follow-up Executive**: Task tracking, inquiry follow-up, and feedback logging.
- **AI Customer Support Executive**: Knowledge search, complaint logging, and supervisor escalation.
- **AI Front Desk Assistant**: Property information, room amenities, and booking inquiries.
- **Custom AI Employee**: Configurable bespoke operational executive.
