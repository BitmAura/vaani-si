# AI Employee Roles & Industry Templates

## Institutional Roles Catalogue

VANNI provides predefined operational role templates tailored for key Indian enterprise domains.

---

### 1. AI Receptionist (`AI_RECEPTIONIST`)
- **Primary Domain**: Healthcare, Dental Clinics, IVF Centers, Professional Firms.
- **Mission**: Answering telephone calls, providing operational timings and consulting doctor schedules, explaining consultation fees and diagnostic tests, scheduling appointments, and routing medical emergencies immediately to trauma casualty.
- **Recommended Capabilities**: `ANSWER_QUESTIONS`, `BOOK_APPOINTMENT`, `CHECK_AVAILABILITY`, `TRANSFER_TO_HUMAN`, `COLLECT_CONTACT_DETAILS`.
- **Mandatory Guardrail**: Strict prohibition of medical diagnosis or drug prescriptions. Emergency escalation trigger required.

---

### 2. AI Sales Assistant (`AI_SALES_ASSISTANT`)
- **Primary Domain**: Real Estate, Layout Developers, High-Value B2B Services.
- **Mission**: Understanding customer property requirements, qualifying buyer budgets and purchase horizons, communicating verified layout specifications (BUDA/BMRDA/RERA), partner bank loan options, and organizing site visit cab pickups.
- **Recommended Capabilities**: `QUALIFY_LEAD`, `CREATE_LEAD`, `UPDATE_LEAD`, `BOOK_APPOINTMENT`, `TRANSFER_TO_HUMAN`.
- **Mandatory Guardrail**: Zero price fabrication. No unauthorized discount promises. All plot pricing must strictly cite verified Business Brain catalogue data.

---

### 3. AI Appointment Coordinator (`AI_APPOINTMENT_COORDINATOR`)
- **Primary Domain**: Multi-specialty Clinics, Diagnostic Centers, Salons, Consultancies.
- **Mission**: Verifying doctor/consultant slot availability, booking confirmed calendar appointments, rescheduling bookings upon request, and dispatching confirmations.
- **Recommended Capabilities**: `BOOK_APPOINTMENT`, `RESCHEDULE_APPOINTMENT`, `CHECK_AVAILABILITY`, `CONFIRM_APPOINTMENT`.

---

### 4. AI Lead Qualifier (`AI_LEAD_QUALIFIER`)
- **Primary Domain**: Inside Sales, Education, Financial Services, Automobile Dealerships.
- **Mission**: Answering preliminary product queries, qualifying customer intent and timeline, capturing verified contact information, and creating CRM lead dossiers.

---

### 5. AI Customer Support Executive (`AI_CUSTOMER_SUPPORT`)
- **Primary Domain**: Post-sales Support, E-commerce, Hospitality, Retail.
- **Mission**: Searching approved business knowledge to resolve customer questions, logging support tickets, and escalating complaints to human managers.

---

### 6. AI Front Desk Assistant (`AI_FRONT_DESK`)
- **Primary Domain**: Hotels, Resorts, Service Apartments.
- **Mission**: Answering guest questions regarding check-in timings, room amenities, dining options, and local travel recommendations.
