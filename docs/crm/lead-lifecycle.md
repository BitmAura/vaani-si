# VANNI Lead Lifecycle & Industry Qualification

This document details the lead progression lifecycle and vertical qualification criteria.

---

## 1. Lifecycle Progression
A lead advances through explicit operational stages:

```
[ NEW ]
   │
   ▼
[ QUALIFICATION ]
   │
   ├───────────► [ FOLLOW_UP ]
   │                 │
   ▼                 ▼
[ APPOINTMENT / SITE VISIT ]
   │
   ▼
[ PROPOSAL / CONSIDERATION ]
   │
   ├───────────► [ CONVERTED ] (Customer Onboarded / Won)
   │
   └───────────► [ LOST ] (Disqualified or Opted Out)
```

---

## 2. Industry Qualification Variations

### A. Dental Clinics
- **Service Type**: Cleaning, Implants, Root Canal, Orthodontics
- **Urgency**: Immediate / Toothache, Routine Cleaning, Weekend Consultation
- **Location**: Specific clinic branch (e.g., Koramangala vs Jayanagar)
- **Preferred Time**: Morning vs Evening slots

### B. Real Estate Developers
- **Project**: Plot layout, Villa, High-rise Apartment
- **Budget Range**: ₹50L - ₹1Cr, ₹1Cr - ₹2.5Cr, ₹2.5Cr+
- **Purchase Horizon**: Immediate (<30 days), 1-3 months, 6+ months
- **Action**: Site Visit with pick-up/reception host

### C. Healthcare & Hospitals
- **Service / Department**: Cardiology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, General Medicine
- **Consultation Type**: In-person, Second Opinion, Tele-consultation
- **Safety Restriction**: AI strictly captures appointment requirements and administrative preferences. AI never diagnoses or prescribes treatment.
