# Karnataka Regional Dialect Architecture

## 1. Dialect Profile Strategy
VANNI does not claim that the initial system models every hyper-local micro-dialect perfectly. Instead, we establish an extensible, profile-driven regional dialect architecture across 6 defined Karnataka regions:

1. **KARNATAKA_GENERAL** (Default statewide standard):
   - Regions: Statewide / All 31 Districts
   - Characteristics: Clean communicative modern Kannada and Kanglish understood uniformly across the state.
2. **BENGALURU**:
   - Regions: Bengaluru Urban, Bengaluru Rural
   - Characteristics: High technical code-mixing, cosmopolitan loanwords, fast cadence.
3. **MYSURU**:
   - Regions: Mysuru, Mandya, Chamarajanagar
   - Characteristics: Rich polite honorifics (*"Thavugaligey"*, *"Kanditha"*), classical grammatical elegance.
4. **NORTH_KARNATAKA (Uttara Karnataka)**:
   - Regions: Hubballi-Dharwad, Ballari, Belagavi, Bagalkote, Vijayapura
   - Characteristics: Distinct spoken verbal suffixes (*"-ri"*, *"-aitri"*, *"helri"*, *"nodri"*).
5. **COASTAL_KARNATAKA (Karavali)**:
   - Regions: Dakshina Kannada, Udupi, Uttara Kannada
   - Characteristics: Melodic Tulu-influenced cadences (*"confirm maduva"*, *"En bekitthu"*).
6. **HYDERABAD_KARNATAKA (Kalyana Karnataka)**:
   - Regions: Kalaburagi, Bidar, Raichur, Koppal, Yadgir
   - Characteristics: Distinct northeastern colloquial terms (*"kam aagbeku"*, *"Haudri"*).
